### Бриф
- Definition：A client intake form on the EMOO website that collects name, contact, company, stand area, and project description; submission triggers an email to emoo@emoo.ru and promises a concept within 24 hours.
- Aliases：brief、stand brief

### EMOO
- Definition：The brand/project name standing for 'Every Moment Of Opportunity'; it is presented as a method (four principles) rather than a simple abbreviation, and is the name of the exhibition stand and event production company behind this site.
- Aliases：E M O O
