### Бриф
- Definition：The lead-capture form on the EMOO website that collects client information (name, contact, company, stand area, message) and emails it to emoo@emoo.ru and tishkova.d@emoo.ru via PHP mail(). The term appears as the form title and subject line prefix `[Бриф на стенд]`.
- Aliases：brief、бриф на стенд

### Выставочные стенды под ключ
- Definition：EMOO's core service offering: end-to-end design, fabrication, and support of exhibition stands, including event production. Used as the primary SEO keyword and page title.
- Aliases：exhibition stand builder、застройка стендов

### Every Moment Of Opportunity
- Definition：EMOO's brand slogan, embedded in the JSON-LD Organization structured data and used as the English tagline.
- Aliases：EMOO slogan

### Площадь стенда
- Definition：A required form field whose accepted values are fixed Russian strings: 'До 50 м²', '50 – 100 м²', '100 – 200 м²', '200 м² и больше', 'Форум / конференция'. The backend normalizes different Unicode forms of the superscript 2 and en-dashes before matching.
- Aliases：stand area、площадь
